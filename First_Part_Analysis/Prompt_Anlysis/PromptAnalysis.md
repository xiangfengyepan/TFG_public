# Prompt Analysis

## Type of Prompt
- SYSTEM: This prompt is designed to provide instructions or guidelines for a system, likely an AI or software application
- PROXY: This prompt is intended to act as an intermediary or representative for another entity, possibly to relay information or perform tasks on behalf of someone else.
- HUMAN: This prompt is meant for human interaction, likely to elicit responses or actions from a person.

**How different types of prompts can help models**
- SYSTEM prompts can help models understand the context and rules they need to follow, ensuring that their responses are aligned with the intended purpose of the system.
- PROXY prompts can assist models in acting as intermediaries, allowing them to perform tasks or relay information effectively, which can enhance their utility in various applications.
- HUMAN prompts can encourage models to generate responses that are more natural and engaging, fostering better communication and interaction with users.
